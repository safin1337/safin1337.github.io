SoulShopBD Payments

Payment receipt importing and reconciliation for SoulShopBD, using its operator-authorized Gmail mailbox.

Read the privacy policy

What the integration does

SoulShopBD Payments imports forwarded payment notifications from the configured Gmail mailbox. It extracts payment details, checks them against submitted orders, and lets authorized staff review receipts that need attention.

The mailbox connection is for the SoulShopBD operator. Customers do not need to connect their Gmail accounts.

Why Gmail access is requested

The integration uses Gmail permission gmail.modify to read payment notifications and update their labels and read status after verification. It keeps messages in their existing inbox or archive location.

This integration does not send, delete, or archive Gmail messages. Google handles sign-in; SoulShopBD does not request your Google password.

Support and account control

For questions about this integration or your data, contact cyanide.eshop@gmail.com.

You can stop future mailbox access through Google account connections. See the privacy policy for storage, how long data is kept, and deletion details.
