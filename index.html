SMS Forwarder Personal

A personal Android integration for sending selected SMS and notification content through your Gmail account.

Read the privacy policy

How the integration works

Choose Use Gmail in the Android app, sign in with Google, and approve the Gmail sending permission. The app uses your selected account to send email to the recipients you configure in its forwarding filters.

Only forward content you are authorized to share. Messages can contain private information, including verification codes.

About this personal setup

This site documents a personal Gmail integration in a modified third-party SMS Forwarder Android installation package, version 10.06.03. It is not the original developer's website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

The repair changes the Gmail sign-in client reference. It does not constitute a complete security or privacy audit of the Android app or its third-party components. This site does not distribute the Android app.

Support and account control

For this personal integration, contact cyanide.eshop@gmail.com. You can stop forwarding in the app and remove its Google account access at any time.
