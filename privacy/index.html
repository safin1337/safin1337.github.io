Privacy policy

How SoulShopBD Payments handles payment-mailbox information.

Effective November 17, 2026

Who operates this integration

SoulShopBD Payments is operated for SoulShopBD's payment receipt importing, verification, and staff review. Contact the operator at cyanide.eshop@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests. This policy covers this Gmail integration and its public information pages.

Google data we access

The operator authorizes a configured payment mailbox using Google's OAuth consent process. The integration requests gmail.modify to read message data and manage labels and read status. This permission is broader than reading alone; the implemented features do not send, delete, or archive Gmail messages.

Accessed information can include the mailbox email address, message identifiers, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, timestamps, message bodies, and labels. Payment notifications may contain names, mobile numbers, wallet identifiers, transaction IDs, and amounts. OAuth access and refresh tokens are authorization credentials that let the server maintain the approved connection. We do not receive your Google password.

How we use the information

The server reads mailbox changes and retrieves message data to find notifications matching the configured sender, recipient, and supported payment formats. It stores imported notification records and extracted receipt details to reconcile payments with orders, prevent duplicate claims, and support authorized staff review.

Verified receipt messages can receive the Gmail label Verified or M.Verified and be marked as read. Inbox placement is preserved. Gmail notification delivery and periodic fetching support importing; a notification alone is not proof of payment.

Access and sharing

Authorized SoulShopBD staff can inspect imported payment information for reconciliation, support, and resolving disputed or ambiguous receipts. Customers receive their own order and payment status, rather than access to the payment mailbox.

Google processes mailbox access and notifications. Hosting and network providers, including the VPS host and Cloudflare, process data needed to operate the service. This public information site is hosted by GitHub Pages; it does not receive Gmail tokens or imported payment records.

We do not sell Gmail user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Our use and transfer of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. This includes its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, protection, and retention

Imported payment information, receipt claims, and review history are stored in the server database. OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in server-only configuration, separate from public website code. Access is limited through server permissions and authenticated staff access. Network connections to Google and the public website use HTTPS.

Payment records are retained for reconciliation, support, and audit history. No fixed automatic deletion schedule is currently applied to those records. Server backups may also contain retained records. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete previously imported data.

Revoke access or request deletion

To stop future Gmail access, remove this application's authorization in Google account connections. The operator can also disable the server's Gmail connection.

To request deletion of stored mailbox tokens, imported payment information, or information you provided for support, email cyanide.eshop@gmail.com. Identify the affected account or transaction without sending passwords or tokens. We may need to confirm your identity and explain any records that must be retained for unresolved transactions, audit requirements, or applicable obligations. We cannot delete copies held independently by Google or other recipients.

This information website

These pages have no sign-in form, advertising or analytics scripts, or browser-storage features. Fonts are served with the site. GitHub and network providers may process technical request information, such as internet addresses and browser details, under their own policies. Emailing support shares the information you include with the operator and relevant email providers.

Changes to this policy

Changes to the integration's data handling will be reflected here with an updated effective date. Material changes to Google data access or use will be disclosed before the changed use begins, with renewed consent where required.
