Privacy policy

SMS Forwarder Personal: the personal Gmail integration and this information website.

Effective November 13, 2026

Scope and contact

This policy describes the personal Gmail integration configured by the operator at cyanide.eshop@gmail.com. It does not replace the original SMS Forwarder developer's policy or describe every component of the modified Android app.

Google account information

Google sign-in provides the account identity needed to select the sender. The integration requests the Gmail sending permission named gmail.send to send messages from that account. That scope does not permit reading or deleting your mailbox.

Google handles sign-in and consent. This website does not ask for or receive your Google password, account access credentials, SMS messages, or Gmail contents.

Message use and sharing

The integration uses Google account information and authorization only to provide the email forwarding and test-email features you choose. Depending on your filters, outgoing email can include SMS or notification content and related sender information.

Outgoing messages are processed by Google and delivered to the email recipients you configure. Choose recipients carefully: forwarded content may include sensitive or one-time verification information.

The operator does not use Google user data from this integration for advertising, sell it, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Human access by the operator is limited to information you choose to share for support and actions you authorize during troubleshooting.

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google services will adhere to the Google user data policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

App settings and any forwarding history enabled in the Android app may remain on the device until you clear them. Email copies are retained by Google and recipients according to their settings and policies. Revoking permission does not delete previously sent email.

The Gmail repair adds no separate server for storing account access credentials or message contents. This statement is limited to the repair; the modified Android app contains other third-party components whose complete data practices have not been independently audited.

Permissions and other components

SMS and notification access depend on the forwarding features enabled in Android. The app can also request Contacts access. Contacts permission was denied in the verified Gmail setup test, and the Gmail repair does not add that permission.

Other app features, embedded third-party components, and the original developer's services may have separate data practices. This policy does not claim that the entire modified app is malware-free or that it collects no data.

This website

These pages contain no sign-in form, advertising scripts, analytics scripts, application database, or intentional browser-storage features. Fonts are served with the website. The hosting provider processes requests and may retain technical logs, such as internet addresses and browser details, under its own policies.

Contacting support by email sends the information you include through the relevant email providers. Do not send passwords, account access credentials, or private SMS contents.

Stop access or delete data

Disable forwarding in the app, remove its authorization from Google account connections, and clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local app data. Delete sent email separately where needed. You can ask recipients to delete their copies; the operator cannot guarantee deletion from recipients' systems.

For questions or deletion requests about information you supplied directly for support, email cyanide.eshop@gmail.com.

Policy updates

Changes to this personal integration or its data handling will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
